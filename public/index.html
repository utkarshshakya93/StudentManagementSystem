Guidance & Support

Frequently Asked Academic Questions

How do I sign in using my Student ID?

Enter your official assigned Student ID (for example, 145493) in the Sign In dialog along with your confidential password. The portal automatically maps your student ID to your verified records.

What happens when I register using Google Sign-In?

Upon your initial Google sign-in, you will be redirected to the Account Settings tab. Other student portal services will remain locked until you complete your mandatory enrollment fields (Student ID, College, Course & Section, Semester, and Security FAQ).

How does the Daily Diary record lecture coursework?

Each daily task entry automatically records the exact date and timestamp, lecture number, subject course code, and category. Entries can be audited and filtered anytime via the Task Log.
